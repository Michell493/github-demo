# LinkedIn "About" Section (Satellite Data Science)

## Version A (Concise)
I’m a satellite data scientist who uses Earth observation data to solve real-world problems in climate resilience, agriculture, water, and disaster response. I build end-to-end geospatial workflows—from data ingestion and preprocessing to machine learning models and decision-ready insights. I work with Sentinel, Landsat, SAR, and large spatiotemporal datasets using Python, Google Earth Engine, and cloud-native tools.

I’m especially interested in translating technical outputs into practical impact for governments, NGOs, and climate-tech teams.

## Version B (Detailed)
I’m a data scientist specializing in satellite and geospatial analytics. My work focuses on transforming remote sensing data into reliable, actionable intelligence for environmental and operational decisions.

I design and deploy workflows for:
- Land cover and change detection
- Crop and vegetation monitoring
- Flood and drought risk mapping
- Urban heat and climate vulnerability analysis

Technically, I combine remote sensing fundamentals with machine learning and geospatial engineering: cloud masking, spectral indices, SAR processing, raster-vector integration, model training/validation, and uncertainty-aware reporting.

Tools I commonly use include Python (GeoPandas, Rasterio, Xarray, Scikit-learn), Google Earth Engine, SQL, and GIS platforms.

I’m open to collaborations and roles where satellite data can drive measurable climate, environmental, and social impact.
